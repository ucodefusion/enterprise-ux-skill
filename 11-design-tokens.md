# Design system and visual consistency

## Tokens
Reuse project tokens for colour, typography, spacing, radius, border, elevation, motion and breakpoints. Do not introduce arbitrary values or duplicate components without reason.

## Hierarchy and density
Use consistent title, section, label and body styles; align content and reduce decorative whitespace without harming readability or touch targets.

## Icons
Use a consistent single-colour outline icon system where appropriate. Label unfamiliar or consequential actions; use tooltips as supplementary guidance.

## Variants and states
Define primary, secondary, tertiary, destructive and contextual variants; cover hover, focus, active, disabled, pending, error and selected states.

## Consistency
Use stable terminology and button positioning. Reuse existing accessible components; document exceptions and support required themes/localisation.
